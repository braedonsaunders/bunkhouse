# AR / collections pilot

The first reference job is the AR / Collections Clerk because it has measurable outcomes, bounded authority, and a natural email audit trail. This is a validation protocol, not a claim of customer proof.

Run the agent against synthetic or parallel-book receivables for at least two weekly cycles. Provide an aging source through a provider-neutral MCP connection, a dedicated mailbox, named escalation owner, approved reminder wording, and a dollar/time budget.

The pilot passes only when every sampled action shows the source invoice and balance, a pinned procedure version, the applicable autonomy decision, the complete mail thread, and token cost. Verify day-1/day-15/day-30/day-60 cadence, suppression after payment, pause and same-day escalation on disputes, promise-to-pay follow-up, manager approval before final notice, and refusal to negotiate discounts or threaten legal action.

Track reminder accuracy, duplicate reminders, disputes mishandled, promises missed, human-review rate, time to response, recovered cash, and cost per account. Record operator sign-off and exceptions; do not promote autonomy until the failure modes are understood. Real-business validation remains outstanding until an operator completes and publishes an anonymized pilot record.
